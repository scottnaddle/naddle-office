## Built-in skills: five in NOTICE, seven in builtin-skills.json

The upstream `NOTICE` is kept verbatim. Its "Built-in skills" section says that `apps/shell/resources/builtin-skills` holds "the five skills shipped with the app" and lists those five third-party skills with their sources and licenses: `meeting-minutes`, `internal-comms`, `humanizer`, `copy-editing` and `find-skills`.

`apps/shell/resources/builtin-skills/builtin-skills.json` lists seven skills. They are the same five third-party skills plus two skills that IeumOffice wrote later and licenses under Apache-2.0: `administration-report` and `administration-report-draft`. Their source in that file is `ieumoffice`. They are not third-party material, so the upstream `NOTICE` skills section does not list them. Both numbers are correct: five third-party skills, seven built-in skills in total. This file does not change the upstream `NOTICE` to say seven.

## Built-in template manifest

`packages/ui/src/authoring/builtin-templates.json` is the built-in template manifest. It is a separate ledger and is not part of the skill count above. It records, for each built-in template, its id, version, license, source, author, locale and, when a form file ships with it, the file path and its sha256.

- Document briefs (`document-brief`), fifteen in all, written by NaddleOffice under Apache-2.0:
  - 견적서, 품의서, 지출결의서, 회의록 and 업무 보고 (S1). These five still have no form file.
  - 기안서, 구매품의서, 출장신청서, 출장보고서, 휴가신청서, 법인카드 사용내역서, 업무협조전, 거래명세서, 발주서 and 공문(시행문) (S3). Each ships a self-authored `.hwpx` form under `packages/ui/src/authoring/assets/` (Apache-2.0, NaddleOffice), and each form includes a 결재란 (approval box with 담당, 검토 and 승인). The files are generated by `packages/ui/scripts/generate-builtin-hwpx-forms.mjs` with the repository's own Hangul support, and the manifest records the sha256 of each committed file. They use generic placeholders only (such as ○○ 주식회사): no real company, approval line or personal data.
  - 공문(시행문) is a structure-only rewrite of a generic official letter (발신 기관명, 분류기호·문서번호, 시행일자, 수신, 제목, 본문, 끝, 발신명의, 붙임·담당). No official form file was copied, including statutory annex forms, and it has no agency logo or official seal (관인).
- Slide designs (`slide-design`): the twelve designs that the upstream `NOTICE` already credits to frontend-slides (https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Zara Zhang, read at commit 9906a34d640d2111f724544cbc50f7f130569ae1). The manifest only records their provenance; the attribution in the upstream `NOTICE` still applies.
- Form skills (`form-skill`): none are built in. This kind is reserved for organization template packs imported by the user.
- Academic slide briefs (`slide-brief`): four briefs for conference presentations, seminars, thesis defenses and grant briefings. Their guidance is adapted from academic-pptx-skill (https://github.com/Gabberflast/academic-pptx-skill, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Gabberflast), read at commit `9f2b703ffe8d1449851617665ab1ffb3516d54ac`. The four ledger entries and `packages/ui/src/authoring/guides/academic/ko.md` and `en.md` carry that adaptation. The upstream root LICENSE is MIT, but the frontmatter of SKILL.md at that commit labels the skill license as Proprietary, so those two statements conflict. ## Tenant template packs

`packages/naddle-template-packs` is a NaddleOffice overlay package (Apache-2.0)
for importing an organization's own template packs on the local PC. It runs in
the main process only. Packs are stored per tenant under the app's user-data
folder (`tenants/<tenantKey>/template-packs/`), not in the personal skill
folder, and they are never uploaded, bundled with the installer, added to the
built-in manifest or synced between machines. There is no server or remote
catalog.

A pack is validated before installation. Scripts, MCP declarations and macros
(including macro-enabled Office files, `vbaProject.bin`, ActiveX, custom UI, OLE
embeddings, external relationships and HWPX `Scripts/`) are refused, and a form
file must match the sha256 the pack declares. The person importing the pack must
confirm the tenant's right to use its forms. The package reuses the upstream
skill import helpers without modifying them.
